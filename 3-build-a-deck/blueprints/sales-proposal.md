## Slides

### 1 — Cover  (pattern: cover-title-center)
- {{proposal title}} · {{your company}} for {{their company}} · {{date}}

### 2 — Agenda  (pattern: agenda-toc)
- {{4-5 sections this deck covers}}

### 3 — Where things stand today  (pattern: key-message-single)
- One sentence naming their situation as you understand it — not a pitch yet, a mirror.

### 4 — What that's costing them  (pattern: chart-with-callout)
- One chart + one callout that quantifies the problem (time, money, risk).

### 5 — Our recommendation  (pattern: key-message-single)
- The one-sentence recommendation (the "so what"). This is the deck's thesis.

### 6 — How it works  (pattern: three-column-icon-card)
- 3 pillars of the solution, each one icon + one line.

### 7 — How we'd roll it out  (pattern: four-step-flow)
- 4 steps from kickoff to live, each with a rough timeframe.

### 8 — What's included  (pattern: pricing-comparison-table)
- Package(s), what's in each, and the price. If there's only one package, use `pricing-plan-cards` instead.

### 9 — Why us  (pattern: three-column-icon-card)
- 3 concrete proof points (not "why we're better than X" — what you've actually done).

### 10 — Risks & how we handle them  (optional, pattern: risk-matrix-2x2)
- Only include if the deal is complex/enterprise enough to expect this question.

### 11 — Next steps  (pattern: action-items-list)
- 3-4 concrete next actions with owners and dates — this is the ask.

### 12 — Closing  (pattern: closing-slide)
- Thank you + contact.

> Tip: one message per slide. Title = the conclusion (an action title). Cut slide 10 for a short/simple deal; add a slide 6b case study (`image-left-text-right`) for a long one.
