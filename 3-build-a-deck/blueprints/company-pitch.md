## Slides

### 1 — Cover  (pattern: cover-title-center)
- {{company name}} · {{one-line tagline}}

### 2 — The point of view  (pattern: key-message-single)
- The one insight that justifies why you exist (not a feature list — a belief).

### 3 — What we do  (pattern: three-column-icon-card)
- 3 things you do, each one icon + one line. Resist listing more than 3.

### 4 — How we work  (pattern: four-step-flow)
- Your process, 4 steps, so it feels concrete instead of vague.

### 5 — Proof  (pattern: three-kpi-big-number)
- 3 numbers that back the pitch (clients, results, speed — whatever's real).

### 6 — A case in point  (pattern: image-left-text-right)
- One story: who, what you did, what changed. Specific beats impressive.

### 7 — The team  (optional, pattern: profile-card-sections)
- Only if the people are part of the pitch (e.g. investor or agency pitches).

### 8 — Closing / the ask  (pattern: closing-slide)
- One clear next step + contact.

> Tip: this is present mode — big type, very few words, the speaker carries the detail. Cut slide 7 for a sales pitch; keep it for an investor or agency pitch.
