## Slides

### 1 — Cover  (pattern: cover-title-center)
- {{period}} · {{company/team name}}

### 2 — Agenda  (pattern: agenda-toc)
- {{sections: headline / trend / breakdown / what's next}}

### 3 — The headline number  (pattern: three-kpi-big-number)
- The 3 numbers that matter most this period, each with its change vs. last period.

### 4 — The trend  (pattern: kpi-bar-chart)
- One metric over time (e.g. last 4 periods) — is it moving the right way?

### 5 — Where it's coming from  (pattern: pie-chart-left-list-right)
- Breakdown of the headline number by segment/channel/product.

### 6 — What drove the change  (pattern: chart-with-callout)
- The one chart + one callout that explains *why* the number moved.

### 7 — Wins & watch-outs  (pattern: summary-three-points)
- 3 short points: what went well, what to watch, what surprised you.

### 8 — What's next  (pattern: action-items-list)
- 3-4 concrete actions for next period, with owners.

### 9 — Closing  (pattern: closing-slide)
- Thank you + questions.

> Tip: keep the skeleton identical every quarter — only the numbers and slide 6/7 commentary change. That consistency is what makes a recurring deck fast to build the 2nd time.
