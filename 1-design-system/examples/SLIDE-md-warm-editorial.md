## Typography

- Display 44pt/700 · H1 36pt/600 · Body 22pt/400 (1.5) · italic accent words in serif.

## Layout
- 16:9 · margin 96px · gutter 24px · generous whitespace · hairline rules (no heavy boxes).

## Do / Don't
- Do: huge serif headline (bottom-left on covers), one accent (sienna), full-bleed photo + scrim.
- Don't: gradients, neon, more than one accent, decorative boxes.
