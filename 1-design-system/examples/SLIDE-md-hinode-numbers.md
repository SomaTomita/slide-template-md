# slide_template.md — hinode-numbers

**Tags:** tone = Bold · use-case = Present / Hybrid
**Reference:** consumer-health & skincare brand · **Use case:** recruiting "by the numbers", company intro, IR highlights — bold and confident

## Colors
| role | name | hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | warm white | #FCFAF3 |
| Secondary | warm near-black | #211D12 |
| Accent | vivid yellow | #F5B700 |
| Ink | warm near-black | #211D12 |
| Muted / hairline | warm gray / line | #9A8F76 / #E9E1CE |

- Light, warm ground with dark text. Vivid yellow is the brand accent — but on a light ground it only works as a **solid fill** (donut arcs, the highlighted bar, a cover accent), not as text.
- For accent text (kicker, the one headline number, icons), use a readable **deep gold** (#8A5E00); vivid yellow text fails contrast on white.
- Big numbers and headings are dark ink. Each slide makes one figure land.

## Typography
- Heading: **Iowan Old Style / Georgia** (serif) · Body: **system-ui**
- Big serif numbers carry each slide; labels are small uppercase.

## Layout
- 16:9 · number-forward · one headline stat or chart per slide · donut rings for percentages, bars for breakdowns.

## Do / Don't
- Do: warm-white ground, vivid yellow for solid fills, deep gold for accent text, large dark serif numbers.
- Don't: put vivid-yellow text on white, use a black background, or use more than one bright color.
