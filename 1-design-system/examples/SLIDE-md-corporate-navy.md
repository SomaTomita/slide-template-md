| role | name | hex |

## Typography
- Heading: **Inter** (or Helvetica/Arial) · Body: **Inter**
- Display 40pt/700 · H1 34pt/600 · Body 22pt/400. Action titles (full-sentence conclusions).

## Layout
- 16:9 · margin 96px · gutter 24px · strict 12-col grid · one chart/table per slide.

## Do / Don't
- Do: navy structure + single teal accent; dense-but-scannable; action titles; appendix for detail.
- Don't: display fonts, bright/rainbow color, decorative templates.
