Step 1 · Set up your design system

Build one slide_template.md.
Reuse it for every slide.

Your design system is a single slide_template.md — colors, fonts, spacing, logo rules. You make it once (from your logo, a reference design, or your own website), then hand it to your AI with each pattern. Below are the reference sources, copy-paste prompts to generate it, and where to paste everything.

In a hurry? Skip this and start from one of ten ready-made systems, or mix your own → — download it, hand it to your AI with ALL-PATTERNS.md, and you're making slides in about two minutes. Build your own (below) when you want it on-brand.

Three ways in, one file out

Pick the source you already have. Whichever you choose, the AI turns it into the same slide_template.md, and that one file then styles every slide.

Design galleries (Dribbble, Land-book, Godly) work too: screenshot a look you like and treat it as Route C.

Route A — Simplest: your logo + colors

The fastest start, no extra tools. Attach your logo, list your brand colors, and let the AI fill in the template. Start here if you're not sure.

Copy-paste prompt 
I'm attaching:
1) slide_template.md — a design-system template (the structure to fill in).
2) screenshot.png — a full-page screenshot of my website (GoFullPage).
3) SLIDE-DESIGN-RULES.md — the rules to follow.

Fill in slide_template.md so it reflects the look-and-feel of screenshot.png.
Keep the template's structure, but set the following from what you see:

- Brand colors (with hex codes) — sample directly from the image.
- Typography (font families, sizes, weights, headings vs body).
- Spacing rhythm (margins, gutters, line-height).
- Logo treatment (size, placement, padding).
- Header / footer layout patterns.
- Recurring components (cards, KPI blocks, comparison sections).

Follow SLIDE-DESIGN-RULES.md: 60-30-10, WCAG AA body >= 4.5:1, max 2
fonts, 8px spacing, 16:9. Output only the finished slide_template.md (markdown).
Off color? Reply: “The screenshot shows a teal-ish primary color near the nav. Sample it more precisely and update the primary hex.”
Check the slide_template.md it returnsReference · six things to verify before you rely on it.
  1. 1Real colors, with #hex. A background, one accent, a text color. Not “blue-ish”.
  2. 2Two fonts at most. One for titles, one for everything else.
  3. 3Says presenting or reading. That decides how much text fits on a slide.
  4. 4Logo, title and page number always sit in the same place.
  5. 5Rules for charts and diagrams. Which color is the highlight, what stays gray.
  6. 6Body text you can read. If it looks faint, reply: “Make the body text darker.”

  1. Get SLIDE-DECK-shell.html (a print-ready, self-contained deck) from “The files” below.
  2. Attach it with your slide_template.md and ask the AI to put one slide per <section class="slide"> — prompt in EXPORT-YOUR-DECK.md.
  3. Open the file: present it (press P; → / Space / click; F = fullscreen), or Print → Save as PDF to share.

Starting from a blank page? Grab a deck blueprint — a ready-made storyline for a sales proposal, a company pitch, a quarterly report, or a research report — and fill in the content.

Printing to PDF? Set Landscape, Margins: None, and turn Background graphics ON — without that last one, the colors print blank. All routes (present / PDF / PowerPoint) are in EXPORT-YOUR-DECK.md.
